# CPI-Inflation
## Overview
This project analyzes **Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation trends in India** using official CPI data. The CPI measures inflation through a fixed basket of goods and services, covering categories such as food & beverages, housing, fuel & light, clothing, medical, education, and recreation.  

The study focuses on **data-driven insights** into inflation patterns, category contributions, and the impact of events like COVID-19 on price levels.

---

## Objectives
- Download and preprocess CPI dataset (monthly index values by category).
- Calculate inflation using the formula:  
- Identify contributions of broader categories (e.g., Food, Housing, Medical).
- Analyze *YoY inflation trends* (2017–2023).
- Investigate food basket inflation (top 3 contributors).
- Study the impact of COVID-19 lockdowns on inflation (housing, education, recreation).
- Examine post-pandemic inflation fluctuations (2021–2023).

## Problem Statements
1. Contribution of categories to CPI (Food, Housing, Transportation, etc.).
2. Trend analysis of CPI growth rates (YoY).
3. Identify peak inflation year and reasons.
4. Food basket analysis (Nov 2021 peak, May 2022–Apr 2023 trends).
5. COVID-19 impact on inflationary trends.
6. Post-pandemic inflation fluctuations and long-term effects.
